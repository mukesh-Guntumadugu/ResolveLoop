Per-user permission flags were re-evaluated during migration. If a feature behaves differently than it did before, contact support with your account ID so we can review your migration record.

Error codes

Quick reference for the messages the Export dialog can show.

CodeMessageWhat it means
EXP-100 No export permission Your user does not have the Export permission enabled, or your role is Viewer. Ask a workspace admin to review your role and permission.
EXP-200 Subscription inactive Your workspace subscription is inactive and the workspace is read-only. A billing contact needs to reactivate it.
EXP-300 Report too large The export exceeds the row, page or size limit. Narrow the report with a filter or split it, then try again.

Export hangs with no code at all? That is different from the three above. Read the export guide or contact support with your account ID and the report name.
