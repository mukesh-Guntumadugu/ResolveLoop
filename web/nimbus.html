Network status

Every line now runs on Core Network Upgrade 5 (CNU-5). The legacy core network was switched off on 31 October 2026.

All systems operational Calls · Texts · Data · Roaming · Nimbus app Updated 11 Nov 2026, 09:00 PT
  1. February – April 2026

    Pilot

    Employee lines and customers who opted in were moved first.

  2. May – September 2026

    General rollout

    All remaining Go, Plus and Pro lines moved in weekly batches by area code, with a text three days before.

  3. 31 October 2026

    Legacy switch-off

    The previous core network was retired. Every active line now runs on CNU-5.

Per-line settings, including the roaming flag, were re-evaluated during the upgrade. If roaming behaves differently than it did before, contact support with your line ID so we can review your upgrade record.

Roaming error codes

Quick reference for the messages the Nimbus app can show under Line › Roaming.

CodeMessageWhat it means
RM-100 Roaming not provisioned Your plan allows roaming, but the roaming flag is not set on your line. This can happen after a network change. Contact support with your line ID; a specialist can re-provision the line.
RM-200 Plan has no roaming add-on Your plan does not include roaming. Add the roaming add-on under Plan › Add-ons, or move to a plan that includes it.
RM-300 Line suspended Your line is suspended, usually for non-payment, and cannot use any network. Settle the balance under Billing; the line returns to Active within 15 minutes.

Partner network shows but there is no code at all? Toggle flight mode, confirm Data roaming is on in your phone settings, then read the roaming guide or contact support with your line ID.
