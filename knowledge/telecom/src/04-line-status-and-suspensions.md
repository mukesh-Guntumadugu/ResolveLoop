# Line status and suspensions

Every Nimbus Mobile line has a **status** that determines whether it can connect to the network at all. This article describes the three statuses, explains why a line is suspended and what that means for calls, texts, data and roaming, shows how to check your status in the Nimbus app, and describes how to get a suspended line working again.

## The three statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | The line is in good standing. Calls, texts, data and any roaming add-on work normally. |
| **Suspended** | The line is temporarily blocked from the network. Nothing works except calls to emergency services and to Nimbus Support. The number and plan are kept. |
| **Inactive** | The line has been closed. The number is released after a holding period and the SIM no longer works. |

## Why a line is suspended

A line can be suspended for one of three reasons. The reason is shown under **Line > Status** in the Nimbus app.

| Reason shown | What happened |
|--------------|---------------|
| *Non-payment* | A bill is unpaid past its due date and the **21-day grace period** has ended. Reminders are sent by text and email during the grace period. |
| *Requested by you* | You asked for the line to be paused, for example after losing your phone. |
| *Under review* | Nimbus paused the line while checking unusual activity, and will contact you. |

Non-payment is by far the most common reason.

## What a suspended line means

- No calls or texts, other than to emergency services and to Nimbus Support.
- No mobile data, at home or abroad.
- Roaming fails with error **RM-300** (*Line suspended*) in the Nimbus app, regardless of your plan or add-ons. RM-300 takes priority over the other roaming codes: a suspended line will never show RM-100 or RM-200.
- Your plan, add-ons, voicemail and number are all retained.
- Monthly charges continue to accrue during a non-payment suspension.

A line suspended for non-payment becomes **Inactive** if the balance is still unpaid **60 days** after suspension. You receive a final notice 14 days beforehand.

## How to check your line status

1. Open the Nimbus app.
2. Tap **Line** at the bottom of the screen.
3. The **Status** card at the top shows *Active*, *Suspended* or *Inactive*, together with the reason if suspended and your line ID (for example `N009`).

If you cannot sign in to the app, the status is also stated in the most recent text or email from Nimbus about your account.

## Reactivating a line suspended for non-payment

Reactivation after non-payment is handled under **Billing**, not under **Line**.

1. Open the Nimbus app and tap **Billing**.
2. The **Outstanding balance** card shows the amount owed and the invoices it covers.
3. Tap **Pay now**, choose or update a payment method, and confirm.
4. Payment is processed immediately. The line returns to **Active** within 15 minutes, and roaming (if included on your plan) is restored at the same time.

If the line still shows *Suspended* 30 minutes after a successful payment, restart your phone. If it persists, contact Nimbus Support with your line ID and the payment reference. Support's Billing team can check that the payment has been applied.

Nimbus Support cannot reactivate a non-payment suspension without the balance being settled, and payment arrangements are agreed with the Billing team, not through the help chat assistant.

## Reactivating a line you paused yourself

Go to **Line > Status** and tap **Resume line**. The line is active again within a few minutes.

## FAQ

**I paid but my line is still suspended.**
Give it 15 minutes and restart your phone. If the status has not changed after 30 minutes, contact Nimbus Support with your line ID and payment reference.

**Will I lose my number if my line is suspended?**
Not while it is suspended. If it becomes Inactive after 60 days, the number enters a holding period and may then be released.

**Can I use roaming while suspended?**
No. A suspended line cannot connect to any network, at home or abroad.
