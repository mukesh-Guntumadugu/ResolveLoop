# Using your phone abroad

When you travel outside your home country, your Nimbus Mobile line connects to a partner network in the country you are visiting. This is called **roaming**. This article explains what has to be in place for roaming to work, how to switch it on before you leave, what a healthy connection looks like once you land, and what the roaming error codes shown in the Nimbus app mean.

## What roaming needs

Three things must all be true for mobile data to work abroad:

| Requirement | Where to check |
|-------------|----------------|
| Your plan includes a **roaming add-on** | Nimbus app > **Plan** > **Add-ons** |
| Your line is **active** (not suspended) | Nimbus app > **Line** > **Status** |
| Roaming is **provisioned** on your line | Nimbus app > **Line** > **Roaming** |

**Provisioned** means that Nimbus has told its partner networks that your line is allowed to connect to them. Provisioning is set on our side when a roaming add-on is active on your plan. It is not the same as the **Data roaming** switch in your phone's own settings, which only tells your handset whether it is allowed to use a foreign network. Both have to be on.

## Before you travel

1. Open the Nimbus app and go to **Plan > Add-ons**. Confirm that **Roaming** shows *Included* or *Added*. If it shows *Not on plan*, see *Plans and add-ons*.
2. Go to **Line > Roaming** and check that the **Roaming** toggle is on and the status line underneath reads *Provisioned*.
3. On your phone, open the mobile network settings and turn on **Data roaming**.
4. Optionally, run **Line > Roaming > Test connection**. The test takes about 20 seconds and reports *Ready to roam* when everything is in place.

## What a normal connection looks like abroad

1. Shortly after you land and take your phone out of flight mode, the signal indicator shows a partner network name.
2. Within a minute you receive a welcome text from Nimbus confirming the country and your roaming allowance.
3. Mobile data, calls and texts work as they do at home.

If the partner network name appears but data never connects, or the Nimbus app shows an error code instead of *Provisioned*, something is wrong on the line rather than on your handset. Note the code and refer to the table below.

## Roaming error codes

| Code | Message shown | What it means and what to do |
|------|---------------|------------------------------|
| **RM-100** | *Roaming not provisioned* | Your plan allows roaming, but the roaming flag is not set on your line. This can happen after a network change. Contact Nimbus Support with your line ID; a specialist can re-provision the line. |
| **RM-200** | *Plan has no roaming add-on* | Your plan does not include roaming. Add the roaming add-on under **Plan > Add-ons** or move to a plan that includes it. |
| **RM-300** | *Line suspended* | Your line is suspended, usually for non-payment, and cannot use any network. See *Line status and suspensions*. |

## Troubleshooting checklist

- Toggle flight mode on and off to force the phone to reconnect.
- Confirm **Data roaming** is on in your phone's settings.
- Check **Line > Roaming** in the Nimbus app for an error code.
- If the app shows **RM-100** even though your plan includes roaming and your line is active, contact support. This is not something you can fix from the app.

## FAQ

**I have the Plus plan. Why does the app say roaming is not provisioned?**
Plus includes the roaming add-on, so the line should be provisioned. If it is not, the flag has dropped on our side. Contact Nimbus Support with your line ID (for example `N004`) and the error code RM-100.

**Does roaming cost extra?**
On Go, the roaming add-on is a paid extra. On Plus and Pro it is included in the monthly price. Usage abroad counts against your plan allowance.

**Can I turn roaming off to avoid charges?**
Yes. Switch the **Roaming** toggle off under **Line > Roaming**, or turn off **Data roaming** on your phone.
