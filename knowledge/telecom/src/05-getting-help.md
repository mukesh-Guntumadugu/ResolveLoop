# Getting help from Nimbus Support

Nimbus Support is here when your line does not behave the way you expect. This article explains how to reach us, when we are available, what to include so that we can resolve your problem quickly, how long you can expect to wait, and how the AI assistant in the Nimbus app hands a conversation over to a person.

## Before you contact us

Many questions are answered in the help center, in particular *Using your phone abroad*, *Core Network Upgrade 5*, *Plans and add-ons* and *Line status and suspensions*. If an article does not solve your problem, or if something that worked before now behaves differently, contact us.

## Ways to contact Nimbus Support

| Channel | Details | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **App chat** | Tap **Help** in the Nimbus app. The AI assistant answers first and can hand over to a specialist. | Quick questions, troubleshooting, anything that might be solved in a few minutes |
| **Email** | support@nimbus-mobile.example | Detailed problems, screenshots, billing questions |
| **Phone** | (555) 010-0288 | Urgent issues during support hours. Free from a Nimbus line, even when suspended. |

Every channel creates a case with a case number; keep it for follow-ups.

## Support hours

Specialists are available **Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 8 pm Pacific**. The AI assistant in the app is available around the clock and can create an email case at any time; a specialist picks it up during the next support hours. Phone and callback are offered during support hours only.

## What to include in your request

1. **Your line ID.** This is the short code shown under **Line > Status** in the Nimbus app, for example `N001`. It identifies your line without you having to read out your phone number. If you cannot open the app, give us the phone number of the line instead.
2. **The error code**, exactly as shown. Roaming errors look like `RM-100`, `RM-200` or `RM-300`. If there is no code and data simply never connects, say so.
3. **Where you are**, if the problem is abroad.
4. **What you expected and what happened**, for example: "The partner network shows, but data never connects and the app shows RM-100."
5. **When it started**, and whether it began after your line was upgraded to Core Network Upgrade 5.
6. **Your plan** (Go, Plus or Pro).

Please do **not** include your account PIN or payment card numbers. We will never ask for your PIN in chat or by email.

## Expected response times

| Plan | App chat (AI assistant) | Email case | Phone and callback |
|------|-------------------------|------------|--------------------|
| Go | Immediate | Within 2 business days | Not included |
| Plus | Immediate | Within 1 business day | Same business day |
| Pro | Immediate | Within 4 business hours | Within 1 hour (priority queue) |

Response time is the time until a specialist first replies.

## How escalation from the AI assistant works

The app chat is answered first by an **AI assistant** that uses this help center and can look up your line's status with your permission. It hands over to a human specialist when:

- it cannot find an answer in the help center,
- the issue needs a change to your line that it is not allowed to make,
- your line is suspended or your billing needs review, or
- you ask for a person.

When it escalates, the assistant offers you **two options**:

1. **Email case.** The assistant creates a case with the chat transcript and your line ID attached, and emails you the case number. A specialist replies within the response time for your plan.
2. **Phone or callback.** During support hours, the assistant gives you the support number, or you can **request a callback** by confirming a number and time window.

The specialist sees everything you told the assistant and continues from there.

## FAQ

**Can the AI assistant re-provision roaming or reactivate my line?**
The assistant can look up your line and explain what it finds. Changes to your line are made by a specialist; the assistant escalates to one.
