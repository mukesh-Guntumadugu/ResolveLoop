# Plans and add-ons

Nimbus Mobile offers three plans: **Go**, **Plus** and **Pro**. On top of your plan you can attach **add-ons** that unlock extra features, the most common being the **roaming add-on**. This article compares the plans, explains how the roaming add-on works, shows how to check what is on your line in the Nimbus app, and describes how to change plans.

## Plan comparison

| | Go | Plus | Pro |
|---|----|------|-----|
| Monthly data at home | 10 GB | 40 GB | Unlimited |
| Calls and texts at home | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Roaming add-on | Available as a paid extra | Included | Included |
| Data abroad | With roaming add-on, from home allowance | 10 GB per month from home allowance | 25 GB per month from home allowance |
| Hotspot | 5 GB | 20 GB | Unlimited |
| Support | App chat and email | App chat, email and phone | App chat, email, phone and priority queue |
| Contract | Monthly, cancel any time | Monthly or 12 months | 12 months |

All plans run on Core Network Upgrade 5 and use the same network at home. The differences are allowance, roaming and support level.

## The roaming add-on

The **roaming add-on** is what allows your line to connect to partner networks abroad.

- On **Go**, roaming is **not included**. You can add the roaming add-on at any time under **Plan > Add-ons** for a monthly fee, and remove it when you no longer need it.
- On **Plus** and **Pro**, roaming is **included** in the plan price and cannot be removed.
- When a roaming add-on is active on your plan, Nimbus **provisions** roaming on your line so partner networks accept it. See *Using your phone abroad* for what provisioning means.
- Roaming usage is taken from the allowances in the table above. Beyond those, data abroad pauses until the next billing period unless you buy a top-up.

Without the add-on, trying to use data abroad shows error **RM-200** (*Plan has no roaming add-on*) in the Nimbus app. This is different from **RM-100** (*Roaming not provisioned*), which means the add-on is present but the line setting is missing.

## How to check your plan and add-ons

1. Open the Nimbus app.
2. Tap **Plan** at the bottom of the screen.
3. The top card shows your plan name, renewal date and remaining allowance.
4. Scroll to **Add-ons**. Each add-on is listed with one of three states:

| State shown | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| *Included* | Part of your plan (Plus and Pro roaming). |
| *Added* | A paid add-on you attached to a Go plan. |
| *Not on plan* | Not active. Tap to add it. |

Your **line ID** (for example `N002`) appears under **Line > Status** and identifies your line when you contact support.

## Adding the roaming add-on to a Go plan

1. Go to **Plan > Add-ons**.
2. Tap **Roaming**, then **Add to plan**.
3. Review the monthly fee and tap **Confirm**.

The add-on is active within a few minutes and roaming is provisioned on your line automatically. Open **Line > Roaming** to confirm it reads *Provisioned*. If, after 15 minutes, it still shows RM-100, contact Nimbus Support.

## Changing plans

You can move between plans under **Plan > Change plan**. Moving up takes effect immediately and is charged pro rata. Moving down takes effect at your next renewal date. Moving from Plus or Pro down to Go removes the included roaming; add the paid add-on if you still need it.

Plan changes do not require a new SIM, and your number and line ID stay the same.

## FAQ

**I moved from Go to Plus. Do I need to do anything to roam?**
No. Roaming is included on Plus and is provisioned automatically. Check **Line > Roaming** before you travel.

**Can I buy roaming for just one trip?**
Yes. On Go, add the roaming add-on before the trip and remove it afterwards. You are charged for the months it was active.

**My plan includes roaming, but the app shows RM-100.**
The add-on is present but the line setting is missing. This has been seen on lines upgraded to Core Network Upgrade 5. Contact Nimbus Support with your line ID; see *Getting help from Nimbus Support*.
