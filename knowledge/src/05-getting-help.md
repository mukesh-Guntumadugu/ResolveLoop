# Getting help from Orbit Support

Orbit Support is here when something in Orbit Reports does not work the way you expect. This article explains how to reach us, when we are available, what to include in your request so that we can resolve it quickly, how long you can expect to wait, and how the AI assistant in the help chat hands a conversation over to a person.

## Before you contact us

Many questions are answered in the help center, in particular *Exporting reports from Orbit Reports*, *Roles and permissions in Orbit Reports*, the *Orbit Cloud 2 migration guide* and *Plans and subscription status*. If an article does not solve your problem, or if something that worked before now behaves differently, contact us.

## Ways to contact Orbit Support

| Channel | Details | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Help chat** | Click **Help** in the bottom-right corner of Orbit Reports. The AI assistant answers first and can hand over to a specialist. | Quick questions, troubleshooting, anything that might be solved in a few minutes |
| **Email** | support@orbit-analytics.example | Detailed problems, attaching screenshots or files, billing questions |
| **Phone** | (555) 010-0199 | Urgent issues during support hours |
| **Callback** | Requested through the help chat when you escalate. A specialist calls you back. | When you prefer to talk but do not want to wait on hold |

Every channel creates a case with a case number; keep it for follow-ups.

## Support hours

Specialists are available **Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Pacific**. The help chat's AI assistant is available around the clock and can create an email ticket for you at any time; a specialist picks it up during the next support hours. Phone and callback are offered during support hours only.

## What to include in your request

Including these details up front saves a round of back-and-forth:

1. **Your account ID.** This is the short code shown under **Workspace settings > General**, for example `C001`. It uniquely identifies your workspace. If you cannot sign in, give us the email address of a workspace admin instead.
2. **The report or dashboard name**, if the problem involves a specific report.
3. **The error code or message**, exactly as shown. Export errors look like `EXP-100`, `EXP-200` or `EXP-300`. If there is no error and the screen simply spins or hangs, say so.
4. **What you expected to happen and what actually happened.** For example: "I clicked Export, chose CSV, and the progress bar has been spinning for five minutes with no download."
5. **When it started**, and whether it worked before. Mention if the problem began around the time your workspace was migrated to Orbit Cloud 2.
6. **Your role** (viewer, analyst or admin), and screenshots if you are writing by email.

Please do **not** include passwords or payment card numbers. We will never ask for your password.

## Expected response times

| Plan | Help chat (AI assistant) | Email ticket | Phone and callback |
|------|--------------------------|--------------|--------------------|
| Starter | Immediate | Within 2 business days | Not included |
| Team | Immediate | Within 1 business day | Same business day |
| Business | Immediate | Within 4 business hours | Within 1 hour (priority queue) |

Response time is the time until a specialist first replies. Issues that need a review of your account record may take longer; we keep you updated through the case.

## How escalation from the AI assistant works

The help chat is answered first by an **AI assistant** that uses this help center and can look up your account's status with your permission.

The assistant hands the conversation to a human specialist when:

- it cannot find an answer in the help center,
- the issue needs a change to your account that it is not allowed to make,
- your subscription or account state means a specialist must review the case, or
- you ask for a person.

When it escalates, the assistant offers you **two options**:

1. **Email ticket.** The assistant creates a case with the full chat transcript and your account ID attached, and emails you the case number. A specialist replies to your email address within the response time for your plan. Choose this if you are not in a hurry or want a written record.
2. **Phone or callback.** During support hours, the assistant gives you the support phone number, or you can **request a callback**: confirm the phone number you want us to use and a time window, and a specialist calls you. Choose this for urgent problems or when a live conversation is easier.

Either way, the specialist sees everything you told the assistant and continues from there.

## FAQ

**Can the AI assistant change my permissions or reactivate my subscription?**
The assistant can look up your account's status and explain what it finds. Changes that need a human review are passed to a specialist through escalation.

**I emailed support but have not heard back. What should I do?**
Check your spam folder for a reply from support@orbit-analytics.example. If nothing has arrived after the response time for your plan, reply to the original email with your case number.

**Can I contact support if I am a viewer?**
Yes. Anyone in the workspace can open a case. For changes to roles, permissions or billing, we may need to confirm the request with one of your workspace admins.
