# Getting help from Harbor Goods Support

Harbor Goods Support is here when an order does not go the way you expect. This article explains how to reach us, when we are available, what to include so that we can resolve your problem quickly, and how the assistant on the website hands a conversation over to a person.

## Before you contact us

Many questions are answered in the help center, in particular *Where is my order?* and *The WMS 4 warehouse cutover*. If an article does not solve your problem, or an order is behaving differently from the way the article describes, contact us.

## Ways to contact Harbor Goods Support

| Channel | Details | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Website chat** | Click **Help** on any page. The assistant answers first and can hand over to a specialist. | Order status, tracking, anything that might be solved in a few minutes |
| **Email** | support@harbor-goods.example | Detailed problems, photos, payment questions |
| **Phone** | (555) 010-0377 | Urgent delivery issues during support hours |

Every channel creates a case with a case number; keep it for follow-ups.

## Support hours

Specialists are available **Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 8 pm Eastern**. The assistant is available around the clock and can create an email case at any time.

## What to include

1. **Your order ID**, for example `H001`, shown under **Account > Orders**. Never send your card number.
2. **The error code**, exactly as shown: `SH-100`, `SH-200` or `SH-300`.
3. **The status** the order shows and how long it has shown it.
4. **What you expected**, for example: "Express order paid Monday, still awaiting dispatch on Thursday with SH-100."

## How escalation from the assistant works

The assistant uses this help center and, with your permission, can look up the status of your order. It hands over to a specialist when it cannot find an answer, when the fix needs a change to your order that it is not allowed to make, when a refund or an address change is involved, or when you ask for a person.

When it escalates, the assistant offers **two options**:

1. **Email case.** A case is created with the transcript and your order ID, and you receive the case number by email.
2. **Phone or callback.** During support hours, the assistant gives you the number above or schedules a callback at a time you choose.

## FAQ

**Can the assistant re-queue a stuck order or issue a refund?**
It can look up your order and explain what it finds. Changes to an order, refunds and address changes are handled by a specialist; the assistant escalates to one.
