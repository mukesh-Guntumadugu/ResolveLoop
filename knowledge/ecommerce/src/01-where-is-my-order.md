# Where is my order?

Every Harbor Goods order moves through a short list of statuses between checkout and your doorstep. This article explains what each status means, where to find your order ID and status in your Harbor Goods account, what the dispatch error codes mean, and when to contact Harbor Goods Support.

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Placed** | We have your order but payment has not been captured yet. |
| **Paid** | Payment is captured and the order is waiting to be released to the warehouse. |
| **Awaiting dispatch** | The warehouse has the order and it is queued for picking, packing and a shipping label. |
| **Dispatched** | A carrier has the parcel; tracking appears under **Orders > Track**. |
| **Delivered** | The carrier confirmed delivery. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled by you or by us and will not ship. |

## Finding your order ID

Open **Account > Orders** on the Harbor Goods website or in the app. Each order shows a short **order ID** such as `H004` together with its status and the delivery tier (**Standard** or **Express**). Quote the order ID, not your card number, whenever you contact us.

## What normal looks like

1. Within a minute of checkout the status moves from *Placed* to *Paid*.
2. Within one business hour the status becomes *Awaiting dispatch* and a **dispatch queued** tick appears under **Orders > Details**.
3. Standard orders are dispatched within two business days, Express orders the same day if paid before 2 pm.

If an order stays in *Awaiting dispatch* for longer than that, open **Orders > Details**. If **dispatch queued** shows a cross instead of a tick, or an error code is shown, the order is stuck on our side rather than with the carrier.

## Dispatch error codes

| Code | Message shown | What it means and what to do |
|------|---------------|------------------------------|
| **SH-100** | *Dispatch not queued* | The order is paid and not cancelled, but the warehouse never queued it, so no shipping label is created. This can happen after a warehouse system change. Contact Harbor Goods Support with your order ID; a specialist can re-queue it. |
| **SH-200** | *Payment not captured* | The order is placed but not paid. Check **Orders > Payment** and retry the payment or update the card. |
| **SH-300** | *Order cancelled* | The order was cancelled and will not ship. Place a new order if you still want the items. |

## FAQ

**My order has been "Awaiting dispatch" for a week.**
Check **Orders > Details** for an error code. If it shows SH-100, contact support with the order ID; this cannot be fixed from your account.

**Will I be charged twice if I reorder?**
No. A cancelled order is never charged. If you were charged for an order that never shipped, support can review a refund.
